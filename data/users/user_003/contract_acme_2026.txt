SERVICE AND SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Page 1

Parties: Northstar Operations Pvt Ltd and Acme Office Systems Pvt Ltd
Effective Date: 2026-09-01
Expiry Date: 2027-08-31

1. Scope
The supplier will provide office furniture and related equipment described in
approved purchase orders.

2. Payment
Invoices are payable within 30 days of receipt and acceptance of the goods.

Page 2

3. Delivery
The supplier must deliver each order by the delivery date stated in the
applicable purchase order.

4. Late Delivery Penalty
If delivery is late, the buyer may apply a penalty of 5% of the delayed order
value for each completed week of delay, subject to a maximum penalty of 15% of
that order value.

5. Disputes
The parties will first attempt to resolve disputes through written notice and
good-faith discussion.
